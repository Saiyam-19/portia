# **Subject: Revision in “Fuel Surcharge on Pilotage” due to changes in fuel prices** 

# Dear Sirs, 

With reference to the Circular no: MIDPL/TC/06/2022 dated 25th May 2022 on 'Fuel Surcharge on Pilotage', the said tariff item was liable for escalation / de-escalation based on the bulk fuel price changes subject to the conditions mentioned there-in. 

As the bulk fuel rate has increased from Rs. 118.5/Litre to Rs. 155.27 /Litre on dated 01-10-2026, the tariff rate has been escalated in the ratio of USD 0.003/GT : Re 1 / Litre change in bulk fuel price. 

With this, the revised tariff rate is as per below table:- 

|**Particulars**|**Bulk Fuel Rate**<br>**(Rs / Litre)**|**Fuel Surcharge**<br>**on Pilotage Rate**<br>**(USD / GT)**|
|---|---|---|
|Base rate<br>(asper Circular no.: MIDPL/TC/06/2022 dated 25th May2022)|118.50|0.100000|
|**Revised rate**|**155.270**|**0.210310**|



The aforesaid revised “Fuel Surcharge on Pilotage” shall be applicable from 0001 Hrs. of dated 01-10-2026
