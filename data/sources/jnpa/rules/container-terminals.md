# NhavaSheva Free Port Terminals (NSFT)

JNPA has signed the concession agreement with M/s. Nhava Sheva Free Port Terminal Pvt. Ltd. (Joint venture between J. M. Baxi & CMA Terminals) on 29th July, 2022   for ‘Upgradation, Operation and Maintenance & Transfer of JNPCT through private sector participation. NSFT has commenced operations from 11th February, 2023.
