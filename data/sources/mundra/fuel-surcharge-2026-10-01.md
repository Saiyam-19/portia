# **Subject: Revision in “Fuel Surcharge on Pilotage” due to changes in fuel prices** 

Dear Sirs, 

With reference to the Circular no: APSEZL/Marine/05/2022 dated 24th May’2022 on 'Fuel Surcharge on Pilotage', the said tariff item was liable for escalation / de-escalation based on the bulk fuel price changes subject to the conditions mentioned there-in. 

As the bulk fuel rate has increased from Rs. 115.28/Litre to Rs. 157.6039/Litre on dated 01-10-2026, the tariff rate has been escalated in the ratio of USD 0.003/GT : Re 1 / Litre change in bulk fuel price. 

With this, the revised tariff rate is as per below table:- 

|**Particulars**|**Bulk Fuel Rate**<br>**(Rs / Litre)**|**Fuel Surcharge**<br>**on Pilotage Rate**<br>**(USD / GT)**|
|---|---|---|
|Base rate<br>(as perCircular no.: APSEZL/Marine/05/2022dated24th May’2022)|115.28|0.100000|
|**Revised rate**|**157.6039**|**0.22696**|



The aforesaid revised “Fuel Surcharge on Pilotage” shall be applicable from 0001 Hrs. of dated 01-10-2026 

Please note that w.e.f. 1st July’2022, the revised tariff rate will only be available on our website under “Trade Notices” section, which can be assessed through below link:
