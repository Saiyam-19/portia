# Mumbai Port Authority

i. Cruise Tariff for a cruise ship shall be recovered @ $0.085 per GRT (fixed rate) and $6 per passenger (Head Tax) for the first 12 hours of stay.

ii. Fixed charge of $0.0175 per GRT per hour on Cruise Ships for the period beyond 12 hours of stay of cruise ship at berth shall be levied.

iii. Volume discounts to Cruise Ships shall be given as under:

| Calls per Year | Rebate |
|-----------------|--------|
| Upto 10 calls | No rebate |
| From 11 to 35 calls | 10% rebate |
| From 36 to 70 calls | 15% rebate |
| Above 70 calls | 20% rebate |
