## CONNECTIVITY

Nhava Sheva International Container Terminal has multimodal connectivity. NSICT is connected to India’s major highways and rail networks, which gives access to neighboring Mumbai and to the hinterland of Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Gujarat, Karnataka and most of North India.

**ROAD:**  
National Highway NH-4B and State Highway SH-54.

**RAIL:**  
DP World Nhava Sheva links to a wide network of inland container depots (ICD) in Pune, Nagpur, Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Madhya Pradesh, Hyderabad, Ludhiana and the NCR Region through two sets of railway sidings, which ensure efficient and successful operations.

* Capacity to handle 6 trains a day, 160 trains monthly.
* 100 trains monthly to the NCR Region (Tuglakabad, Loni, Dadri, Faridabad, Patli, Garhi Harsaru)
