# APM Terminals Pipavav
## Circular No. GPPL/MAR/01/2026
Date: 10th June 2026
To: All Shipping Agents / Internal Departments, APM Terminals Pipavav

Subject: Mandatory Submission of FAL Forms through Maritime Single Window (MSW 2.0 – Sagar Setu)

As per DG Shipping Circular No. 29 of 2026 dated 08.06.2026, and in continuation of MS Notice No. 01 of 2024 & GPPL Circular No. GPPL/MAR/01/2024, all stakeholders are hereby informed that submission of FAL forms electronically through the Maritime Single Window (MSW 2.0 – Sagar Setu portal) is mandatory for vessel arrival and departure clearance at Pipavav Port with effect from 15th June 2026.

The Master of the vessel or the Authorized Agent must electronically submit all seven FAL forms:

- General Declaration (FAL Form 1)
- Cargo Declaration (FAL Form 2)
- Ship's Stores Declaration (FAL Form 3)
- Crew's Effects Declaration (FAL Form 4)
- Crew List (FAL Form 5)
- Passenger List (FAL Form 6)
- Dangerous Goods Manifest (FAL Form 7)

### Key Instructions

- All FAL forms must be submitted through MSW 2.0 by shipping agents/ship masters/authorized representatives.
- Pilot Booking, Berth Allotment, and related port operational clearances will only be processed after confirmation of submission of requisite FAL forms through MSW 2.0, except in emergency or safety-related circumstances.
- Submissions should be completed sufficiently in advance, preferably not less than six hours prior to vessel entry/arrival.
- Parallel submission via email/manual mode is to be discontinued immediately, except in cases of temporary system contingencies.
- In case of technical issues, shipping agents must attempt submission through MSW 2.0 and share screenshots/details with concerned authorities.
- For technical support, contact the 24x7 MSW Sagar Setu helpline: Toll-free 1800-270-5260 or email sagarsetu.support@ipa.org.in.

All departments and shipping agents are requested to accord priority to this matter and ensure strict compliance with DGS Circular No. 29 of 2026, dated 08.06.26 for effective implementation of the Maritime Single Window framework.
