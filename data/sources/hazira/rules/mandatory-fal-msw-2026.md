# Adani Ports and Logistics

Date: 11th June' 2026

To, Shipping Agents / Lines, Adani Hazira Port Ltd.

## Subject: Mandatory Submission of FAL Forms through Maritime Single Window (MSW) System

Dear Sirs,

This is to inform you that, as per appended DGS Circular No. 29 of 2026 dated 08.06.2026, issued by the Directorate General of Shipping, Government of India, the submission of FAL Forms has been made mandatory through the Maritime Single Window (MSW Version 2.0).

Submission of all applicable FAL Forms for vessel arrival and departure shall be carried out only through MSW portal with effect from 15th June 2026.

All stakeholders are requested to strictly comply with the above instructions to ensure seamless vessel clearance processes and successful implementation of the Maritime Single Window System.

Pilot will be assigned only after confirmation of requisite FAL Forms through MSW.
