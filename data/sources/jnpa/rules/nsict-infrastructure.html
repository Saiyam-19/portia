DP WORLD NHAVA SHEVA INTERNATIONAL CONTAINER TERMINAL  INFRASTRUCTURE

India's gateway to global trade, delivering world-class container handling capacity, advanced automation, and strategic Mumbai connectivity

    DP WORLD NHAVA SHEVA TERMINAL INFRASTRUCTURE

    KEY HIGHLIGHTS

    State-of-the-art IT infrastructure, technology and support specifically tailored to facilitate the smooth flow of traffic and transactions

    TERMINAL SPECS

    QUAY SIDE
    Cranes 2x NOELL Super post Panamax single lift - outreach 50 m
    2x ZPMC Super post Panamax twin lift - outreach 50 m
    4x ZPMC Super post Panamax twin lift, high capacity - outreach 55 m
    Backreach 15 m from landside rail
    Rail Gauge 20 m
    Lifting Height below wharf 17 m
    Lifting Height above wharf NOELL 34 m, ZPMC (2 nis) 38 m, ZPMC (4 nos) 40 m
    Safe Working Load under Spreader NOELL 40 m, ZPMC 50 m (twin 55/60)
    Safe Working Load under Cargo Beam NOELL 55 m, ZPMC 70 m

     

    YARD SIDE  
    RTGs 11 NOELL
    15 KALMAR diesel
    Reach Stackers 2x 40 tonne SWL
    Empty Handlers 2x 8 tonne SWL
    Forklifts 2x 3 tonne

    CONNECTIVITY

    Nhava Sheva International Container Terminal has multimodal connectivity. NSICT is connected to India’s major highways and rail networks, which gives access to neighboring Mumbai and to the hinterland of Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Gujarat, Karnataka and most of North India.

     

    ROAD:
    National Highway NH-4B and State Highway SH-54.

     

    RAIL:
    DP World Nhava Sheva links to a wide network of inland container depots (ICD) in Pune, Nagpur, Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Madhya Pradesh, Hyderabad, Ludhiana and the NCR Region through two sets of railway sidings, which ensure efficient and successful operations.

    • Capacity to handle 6 trains a day, 160 trains monthly.
    • 100 trains monthly to the NCR Region (Tuglakabad, Loni, Dadri, Faridabad, Patli, Garhi Harsaru)
