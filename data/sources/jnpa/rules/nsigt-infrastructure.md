## CONNECTIVITY

Nhava Sheva International Gateway Terminal is connected to India’s major highways and rail networks, which gives access to neighbouring Mumbai and to the hinterland of Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Gujarat, Karnataka and most of North India.

**ROAD:**  
National Highway NH-4B and State Highway SH-54.

**RAIL:**  
DP World Nhava Sheva links to a wide network of inland container depots (ICD) in Pune, Nagpur, Ahmedabad, Hyderabad, Ludhiana and the NCR Region through two sets of railway sidings, which ensure efficient and successful operations.

* Capacity to handle 6 trains a day, 160 trains monthly.
* 100 trains monthly to the NCR Region (Tuglakabad, Loni, Dadri, Faridabad, Patli, Garhi Harsaru)

**WATER:**  
We offer key connectivity to SouthEast Asia, Indonesia, China, UAE and the Pacific.
