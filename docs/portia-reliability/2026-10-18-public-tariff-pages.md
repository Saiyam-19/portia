### ICTPL derivation and review

The [2016 regulator record](https://www.abmp.gov.in/uploads/tariff_orders_master/order_6a451062dd5e9.pdf), physical page 3, separately identifies the then-applied RoRo berth base as USD0.0092/GRT/hour and the proposed higher rate. Only the historical base is used. It matches the contemporaneous Indira Dock/Ballard row, whose alternative Princes/Victoria row was USD0.0073. The 2018 approved OCT table then specifies 1.3 times applicable MbPT berth hire, minimum 1,000 GRT except offshore supply vessels, and hours or parts. Subsequent SOP clauses record 130% practice, including admitted non-RoRo cargo ships.

Current ordinary base amounts, from the table effective9May 2026, are USD0.0137 and INR0.2165. Multiplication by 1.3 gives USD0.01781 and INR0.28145. This is a **source-chain inference**. The 2018 approval has an expiry provision, SOP 5.2 records 100% and 130% alternatives, and the December 2025 extension of an operational SOP does not expressly renew the expired tariff approval. Do not erase those limits. Readiness-to-sail, penalties and other duration exceptions also remain applicable where established; a simple hourly product is not a complete invoice.

A separate read-only reviewer checked the chain and arithmetic. Parent visually checked the material 2016, 2018 and current wharfage tables and refreshed the live tariff index.
