Its identified instrument is [S.O. 3641(E), 28 July 2022](https://www.dgll.nic.in/sites/default/files/2022-12/Published%20Notification_Marine%20Aids%20to%20Navigation%20Dues.pdf), Gazette publication 2 August 2022. [DGLL FAQ](https://www.dgll.nic.in/faqs) records implementation from 1 September 2022 and payment validity for 30 days. Do not infer instrument expiry from a webpage metadata field or automatically bill this appendix again at each port. Before activation verify vessel class, NT/TEU basis, exemptions, receipt validity, taxes and amendments. BPCL liquid also includes the appendix twice; one national charge must not become two line items.
