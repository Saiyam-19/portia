# 2. General Conditions

* Fractions of a Rupee shall be rounded **up to the next full Rupee**.
* Charges notified in Indian Rupees shall apply to:

  * Foreign-going vessels
  * Indian vessels engaged in foreign trade
* Charges shall be collected from vessel owners in Indian Rupees.

## Units of Measurement

| Measurement           | Definition                 |
| --------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Weight                | 1 Metric Tonne (1,000 kg)  |
| Volume (bulk liquids) | 1 Kilolitre (1,000 litres) |

### Cargo Declaration

* The party filing the Import/Export Application is responsible for declaring the correct unit(s).
* If shipping documents do not specify units, the cargo must be weighed or measured before filing.
* Any under-declaration discovered during verification will attract penalties.

### Payment

* Payments shall be made via:

  * Crossed Demand Draft
  * Banker's Cheque
  * Online Transfer
* Payable to:

> **JSW JNPT Liquid Terminal Private Limited**
> Mumbai

### Other Conditions

* Charges payable directly to **JNPA** (Pilotage, Towage, Port Dues, Berth Hire, Anchorage, etc.) shall be as per JNPA's published tariff.
* Scale of Rates may change without prior notice.
* JJLTPL's interpretation of these rates shall be final.
* Terminal provides **wharf facilities only**.
* Pumping in/out through pipelines must be arranged by importers/exporters through tank farm operators registered with JNPT.
* Liquid bulk cargo handling is permitted **only through pipelines**.
* Cargo assessment shall be based on:

  * Bill of Entry / Coastal Bill of Lading (Imports)
  * Shipping Bill (Exports)

# 3. Tariff for Liquid Cargo

## A. Cargo Handling Charges

| Particulars            | EXIM (including Coastal POL & Crude Oil) | Coastal *(Except POL & Crude Oil)* |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------: | ---------------------------------: |
| Cargo Handling Charges |                         **₹252.00 / MT** |                   **₹151.20 / MT** |

### Notes

* Coastal cargo of POL (including crude oil) does **not** fall under MoPS&W Communication No. PR-14019/29/2001-PG dated 1 January 2005.
* Charges are recovered from the importer/exporter based on manifested quantity.
* Charges are payable in Indian Rupees.
* GST / Service Tax / Other applicable taxes, duties, levies, cess, surcharge, etc. are extra.
* Applicable till **31 March 2025**.
