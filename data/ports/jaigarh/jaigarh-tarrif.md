## J-15 Cold Move Charges (in USD)

1. Planned cold move for berthing of a vessel is carried out only with prior approval from the concerned agency; however, no prior approval is necessary for cold move of a vessel already at berth. The Port's decision to vacate the berth (to accommodate a waiting vessel) is final. Charges for each berthing/un-berthing/shifting process are in addition to pilotage charges, as follows:
   - **3 times** the pilotage charges (subject to minimum clause).
   - All tugs used for the planned cold move are charged extra on an hourly basis per Port tariff.
2. Whenever engine/steering gear fails during berthing/un-berthing/shifting/warping, it is treated as a cold move and charged at **1.5 times** the normal pilotage charges (subject to minimum clause). Any additional tug(s) used due to the failure are charged per Port tariff on an hourly basis. Cold move charges are over and above normal pilotage and towage charges.

## J-16 Non Berth Vacating Charges (in USD)

| Item | Rate |
|---|---|
| Non Berth Vacating Charges | 2 times the berth hire per Port Tariff (minimum berth hire charges apply). In addition to applicable berth hire. |

**Applicable when:**
- Vessel is unable to commence cargo operations within 2 hours of All Fast time (for Molasses/Sugar vessels, 2 hrs. is read as 4 hrs.)
- Vessel is not ready to sail (i.e., fails to book outward pilot memo) within 2 hours from completion of cargo (for Molasses/Sugar vessels, 2 hrs. is read as 4 hrs.)
- Vessel discontinues cargo operations (loading/discharging) for its own reasons

**Port will NOT charge Non Berth Vacating charges** on a vessel remaining at berth after cargo completion if receiving bunker/freshwater/waste collection, subject to:
1. No vessels waiting for that berth.
2. No planned maintenance activity on that berth by the Port.
3. Request for additional berth stay made at least 3 hours prior to cargo completion, so other vessel movements at other berths aren't adversely affected.
4. The vessel gets a pilot only after other planned vessel movements are carried out (efforts will be made not to unduly delay these vessels).

*Any planned stay at berth after cargo completion may be permitted at regular berth hire charges, provided the request is made to the Marine Dept. in writing at least 2 hrs. before cargo completion. Port has full right to accept/reject such requests at its discretion.*
