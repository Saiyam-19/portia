# **<u>Subject: Fuel Surcharge due to recurrent hike in the fuel prices</u>** 

Dear Sirs, 

Mundra Port has always been a trusted partner to all stakeholders, and we value the deep-rooted partnership with all valued clients 

The recurrent increase in the fuel prices since beginning of this year 2022, has resulted into cascading effect on entire operational expenses and has increased our operating cost steeply, and same is posing a stiff challenge to us. 

For the continuity of the smooth and uninterrupted operation at Mundra port, we have introduced “Fuel Surcharge on Pilotage”, in addition to the regular pilotage charges, as “ **USD 0.1 / GT”** for all the vessel coming at Mundra port. 

We will review the aforesaid Fuel Surcharge, on fortnightly basis, which is subject to following: - 

1. Escalation / de-escalation in fuel-surcharge: Any cumulative increase or decrease in bulk fuel rate less than Re 1 / litre will not have any change in the fuel surcharge, however any cumulative increase or decrease of Re 1 / Litre or more in bulk fuel rate will have an increase or decrease in the ratio of Re 1 / Litre: USD 0.003 / GT. Example: - 

|**Scenario**|**Bulk Fuel**<br>**Rate**<br>**(Rs / Litre)**|**Increase /**<br>**Decrease**<br>**(USD / GT)**|**Fuel Surcharge**<br>**on Pilotage**<br>**Rate**<br>**(USD / GT)**|
|---|---|---|---|
|Base rate|115.28|-|0.1000|
|(Fuel Surcharge on Pilotage)||||
|Scenario 1 Revised rate|116.28|+ 0.003|0.1030|
|Scenario 2 Revised rate|114.28|- 0.003|0.0970|



2. The de-escalation in “Fuel Surcharge on Pilotage” is subject to maximum of USD 0.1/GT (i.e. base tariff rate) and hence the revised fuel surcharge rate cannot be negative. 

3. The base price for bulk fuel rate for this purpose is the total landed price at Mundra which is currently at Rs 115.28/Litre.
