In partial modification of the Office Circulars referred to above, the Competent Authority has approved the following amendment to the Comprehensive Berthing Policy relating to the 24-Hour Priority Category under the Order of Allotment for Dry Cargo.

### (i) 24-Hour Priority Category

The existing eligibility criterion requiring a vessel to complete cargo operations within 24 hours is hereby modified.

The revised provision shall be read as under:

"Any vessel requiring berth occupancy for carrying out cargo operations for a maximum period of twenty-four (24) hours shall be eligible for berthing under this category, irrespective of parcel size.

The vessel shall vacate the berth in the same tide on the day following the day of berthing. In all cases, the total stay at berth shall not exceed twenty-four (24) hours or till the last available sailing pilot in the same tide. The vessel shall mandatorily vacate the berth in accordance with the prevailing tidal conditions.

Allotment of berth under this category shall be based on seniority."
