Jaigarh Port, JSW Infrastructure's flagship and largest port by capacity, is strategically located between Mumbai and Goa. Designed for multipurpose operations, it facilitates seamless cargo movement, optimises logistics efficiency, and anchors trade flows across India's western coast with world-class infrastructure and operational excellence.
